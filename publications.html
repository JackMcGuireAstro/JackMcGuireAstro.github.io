Publications

  • Broad-band Monitoring of 797 Montana

    Bentz, M. C.; Alahakone, R.; Azoulay, L.; Burns-Kaurin, E.; Chaturvedi, A. S.; Davis, M.; Kimani-Stewart, K.; Markham, M.; McGuire, J. P.; McMullan, E.; Patel, M.; Binu Raj, M.; Sankey, D.; Wilson, A. A.-L.; Zdanky, K. (2025). “Broad-band Monitoring of 797 Montana.” Minor Planet Bulletin, 52(3), 207–208.

    A four-night photometric campaign on the main-belt asteroid 797 Montana using the 24-inch Miller telescope at Hard Labor Creek Observatory, giving a rotation period of 4.545 ± 0.001 hr and an amplitude of ΔV = 0.50 ± 0.03 mag.
