Evidence pipeline

  1. Publication or catalog change discovered
  2. Linked to a source and object identity
  3. Measurements normalized with units and uncertainty
  4. Validated against scientific and release gates
  5. Promoted into a versioned evidence release

What “automatically updated” means

Provider checks and validated static publication are automated. A newly discovered paper is not silently converted into a trusted measurement: it must pass a source-specific identity, units, uncertainty, provenance, and regression contract. Failed providers are recorded while the previous validated measurements remain available.

Scientific boundary

Catalog projections, publication-specific measurements, source composites, limits, candidates, and confirmed detections remain distinct. WorldsIndex never treats archive membership as independent confirmation or silently averages incompatible solutions.
