Three terms that prevent most confusion

Confirmed means the object satisfies a catalog’s validation or confirmation criteria; it does not make every published number agree.

Candidate means a signal may be planetary but still needs validation or follow-up.

Measurement is one source’s reported result. Different instruments, models, or papers can produce different values for the same property.

Scientific and automation boundary

The laptop updater can repeatedly query structured catalogs, validate changes, quarantine unsafe releases, and publish a last-known-good static release. No website can honestly guarantee every future paper or provider forever: services change schemas, restrict access, disappear, or publish measurements only in unstructured literature. Every declared provider therefore remains visible with its exact implementation and latest-query state instead of being silently counted as complete.

  • A catalog row is not independent confirmation.
  • An archive holding is not itself a planet detection.
  • Confidence describes evidence fitness and provenance, not the probability that a value is true.
  • Source-composite values may come from different papers and are not a self-consistent physical solution.
  • “All” means the validated records inside the versioned declared source universe shown here.
