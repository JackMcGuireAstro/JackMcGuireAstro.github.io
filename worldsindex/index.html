Scientific and hosting boundary

GitHub Pages serves the complete frozen public release directly. It cannot safely hold private credentials or run arbitrary secret-backed provider queries. Those providers remain declared with their exact state, scope, limit, and official link; future measurements enter through the guarded refresh pipeline and become static, inspectable release artifacts after validation.

  • A catalog row is not independent confirmation.
  • An archive holding is not itself a planet detection.
  • Confidence describes evidence fitness and provenance, not the probability that a value is true.
  • Source-composite values may come from different papers and are not a self-consistent physical solution.
  • “All” means the validated records inside the versioned declared source universe shown here.
